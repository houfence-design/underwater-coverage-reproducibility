# Figure and table data provenance

The environmental input is the actual public GLORYS12V1 one-degree derivative supplied by Mercator Ocean International. Downloaded environmental records are never generated by this project. The retained-depth NetCDF files, original metadata, public object URLs and SHA-256 values are archived in data/cmems. The only interpolation is linear interpolation between two original depth levels to 150 m. There is no temporal interpolation, replacement or missing-date imputation.

Monte Carlo pseudorandom numbers are integration/sampling variables generated from explicitly assumed laws with archived seeds. Files named mc_raw hold the computed Bernoulli event outcomes, not original environmental observations. They do not replace real current inputs or supply empirical evidence that the assumed position-error distribution is calibrated. No CFD, LSTM, sea-trial sensor position or measured energy dataset is asserted to exist.

Environmental conclusions use the public-data replay. Numerical benchmark figures retain the original paper's analytical topics and serve implementation checks. Their results are explicitly limited to the stated model. Download and verification scripts reject missing or inconsistent environmental files rather than synthesizing replacements.
