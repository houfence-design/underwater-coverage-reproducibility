# Reference verification

The original bibliography contains 21 records. Seventeen relevant, identifiable original references are retained after bibliographic correction. Four title/author/DOI combinations do not match the registered work and are not used as supporting citations. Eight methodological references and three ocean-data references are added, producing 28 final references.

The original DOI registry responses are frozen in data/reference_records.json. The complete original-to-corrected record comparison is retained locally in the Chinese audit. A bibliographic match does not substantiate claims beyond the cited work's scope.

The four mismatched original DOIs are 10.1016/j.oceaneng.2023.115432, 10.1016/j.oceaneng.2023.115487, 10.1016/j.swevo.2023.101305 and 10.1016/j.oceaneng.2018.11.046. They were not reassigned to fabricated titles. See the complete source records for their registered titles.
